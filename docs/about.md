As a young man, I found so many things interesting that I didn't go very far in any direction and was, 
as many young people are I suppose, rather aimless. Len Herzenberg was going somewhere and I followed 
along. I learned along the way that I was a more effective follower than a leader. I had been interested 
in geometry since high school, so very early on I asked myself the question of whether it was possible 
to bring the tools of geometry to bare on flow cytometry. Now, at the end, I can answer in the affirmative. 
Exhaustive Projection Pursuit is an application of the methods of computational geometry, and Covariant 
Statistics an application of differential geometry, to the analysis for flow cytometry data.

May his memory be a blessing.

Wayne A. Moore
